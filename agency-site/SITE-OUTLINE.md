# Marketing Agency Website: Structure, Copy and Content Strategy

> **Brand-neutral blueprint.** Wherever a name is needed, the copy uses `[Agency]`. Numbers in `[brackets]` are placeholders. **Replace them only with real, documented figures.** Case studies and testimonials are templates; publish them only with client permission and verifiable results.

## 1. Positioning and messaging foundation

**Positioning statement**
For growth-stage and established businesses that need marketing to show up in revenue, not just reports, `[Agency]` is a full-service marketing partner. It combines brand strategy, performance media and analytics under one accountable team. Unlike agencies that sell activity, it sells measurable outcomes and reports every result against an agreed KPI.

**Brand voice**
Confident, plain-spoken and evidence-led. Short sentences, specific numbers and no hype words ("revolutionary", "best-in-class"). Every claim is either proven on the page or not made.

**Core value propositions** (repeated site-wide)

| # | Value proposition | Proof mechanism on site |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Results you can audit.** Every engagement starts with agreed KPIs and a live dashboard. | Sample dashboard screenshot, reporting page |
| 2 | **Strategy and execution under one roof.** Brand, media, content, SEO, PR and analytics work as one team. | Services map, team page |
| 3 | **Senior people on your account.** No hand-off to juniors after the pitch. | Named leads with years of experience on each service page |
| 4 | **Transparent spend.** Media budgets are billed at cost, and fees are fixed or tied to performance. | Pricing-model explainer and FAQ |
| 5 | **Speed to first result.** A 30-day launch plan with a 90-day performance review. | "How we work" timeline |

**Hero messaging options** (A/B test the top two)
1. **"Marketing that answers to revenue."** Sub: Strategy, creative, media and analytics run by one accountable team, measured against the numbers that matter to your business.
2. **"Grow with evidence, not guesswork."** Sub: We plan, launch and optimise campaigns across every channel and show you exactly what each one returns.
3. **"From brand to bottom line."** Sub: Full-service marketing for companies that expect every campaign to earn its budget.

**Primary CTA:** *Book a free growth audit*
**Secondary CTA:** *See our results*
**Low-commitment CTA:** *Download the 2026 Marketing Benchmark Report*

## 5. Services portfolio

Each service page uses **the same template** so it's easy to scan and consistent for SEO:

> Hero (outcome headline + sub + CTA) → Who it's for → What's included (deliverables) → Our process (3–5 steps) → Tools & platforms → **Proof** (1–2 case-study snippets + metric) → FAQ (4–6, with FAQ schema) → Related services → CTA band

### 5.1 Brand Strategy
- **Headline:** Build a brand people remember and choose.
- **Description:** We define who you are, who you serve and why you win, then turn that into a brand system your whole team can use.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Market and competitor research, audience personas
  - Positioning and messaging framework
  - Brand architecture, verbal identity and tone of voice
  - Visual identity direction and brand guidelines
  - Launch or rebrand roll-out plan
- **Expertise shown:** a sample positioning canvas (anonymised), a before/after messaging example and a brand-tracking metric (awareness, consideration).
- **KPIs:** aided/unaided awareness, branded search volume, share of voice, conversion-rate lift on brand-led pages.

### 5.2 Digital Marketing
- **Headline:** One digital strategy across every channel, measured as one.
- **Description:** Integrated planning across search, social, email, content and marketplaces, with budget allocated to whatever drives the lowest cost per outcome.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Channel strategy and media plan
  - Funnel mapping, email and marketing automation
  - Landing page and CRO programme
  - E-commerce and marketplace growth
  - Monthly growth reporting
- **Expertise shown:** an anonymised full-funnel diagram, plus a channel-mix chart from a real (permissioned) campaign.
- **KPIs:** revenue, ROAS, CAC, conversion rate, LTV:CAC ratio.

### 5.3 Advertising (paid media)
- **Headline:** Ads that earn their budget.
- **Description:** Planning, buying, creative and optimisation across Meta, TikTok, Google, YouTube, LinkedIn, programmatic display and out-of-home.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Media planning and buying, audience strategy
  - Creative production and testing frameworks (hooks, formats, offers)
  - Pixel/CAPI and conversion tracking set-up
  - Daily optimisation and budget pacing
  - Weekly reporting
- **Expertise shown:** a creative-testing matrix, a "what we learned from [X] ad variations" panel and a ROAS trend chart.
- **KPIs:** ROAS, CPA, CPM, CTR, thumb-stop rate, incremental lift.

### 5.4 SEO and SEM
- **Headline:** Be found when buyers are searching.
- **Description:** Technical SEO, content and authority building for long-term organic growth, plus paid search for immediate demand capture, planned together so they never compete.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Technical audit (Core Web Vitals, indexation, schema)
  - Keyword and intent research, on-page optimisation
  - Content clusters, digital PR and link earning
  - Local SEO
  - Google Ads and Microsoft Ads search and shopping campaigns
  - Search-term mining and bid strategy
- **Expertise shown:** organic traffic growth curve, keyword-ranking table and a paid/organic overlap analysis.
- **KPIs:** organic sessions and conversions, rankings for money keywords, impression share, CPC, search ROAS.

### 5.5 Social Media
- **Headline:** Social that builds community and drives sales.
- **Description:** Strategy, content, community management and influencer partnerships on the platforms your audience actually uses.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Platform strategy and content pillars, monthly calendars
  - Short-form video production, community management
  - Influencer and creator programmes
  - Social listening, monthly insights
- **Expertise shown:** a content-pillar framework, an engagement benchmark against industry averages and a sample calendar.
- **KPIs:** engagement rate, follower quality/growth, reach, saves and shares, social-sourced traffic and sales.

### 5.6 Public Relations
- **Headline:** Earn attention you can't buy.
- **Description:** Media relations, thought leadership and reputation management that build credibility with press, partners and customers.
- **Deliverables:**
  - PR strategy and narrative, press releases and media kits
  - Journalist outreach, founder and executive thought leadership
  - Crisis and reputation planning, award entries
  - Coverage reporting
- **Expertise shown:** a coverage wall (with permission), a share-of-voice chart and a narrative framework example.
- **KPIs:** earned coverage (tier-weighted), share of voice, referral traffic, backlinks from coverage, sentiment.

### 5.7 Promotions and campaigns
- **Headline:** Launches and promotions that move product.
- **Description:** Product launches, seasonal campaigns, offers, loyalty programmes and on-ground activations, planned end to end and measured to the sale.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Campaign concept and creative, offer and pricing strategy
  - Launch plan and timeline, retail and partner promotions
  - Event and activation management
  - Post-campaign analysis
- **Expertise shown:** a launch timeline Gantt chart, a promo-lift analysis and redemption-rate data.
- **KPIs:** incremental sales, redemption rate, new-customer share, campaign ROI.

### 5.8 Analytics and measurement
- **Headline:** Know what's working, and why.
- **Description:** Tracking, dashboards, attribution and experimentation so every marketing decision is backed by data you trust.
- **Deliverables:**
  - GA4 and Tag Manager set-up and audits, server-side tracking
  - Looker Studio or Power BI dashboards
  - Attribution and marketing-mix modelling
  - A/B testing and CRO programme, cohort/LTV analysis
- **Expertise shown:** an interactive sample dashboard (demo data, clearly labelled), a measurement plan template and a test-result case.
- **KPIs:** tracking accuracy, data coverage, test velocity and win rate, decision cycle time.

## 6. Case studies

**Listing page:** a filter bar (service · industry · goal), a card grid (industry tag, headline metric, 1-line challenge, thumbnail) and a CTA band.

**Case-study template** (keep it short and skimmable):

1. **Headline with the result:** "How a `[industry]` brand cut CAC by `[X]`% in 90 days"
2. **Snapshot box:** client (or anonymised descriptor) · industry · services · timeline · **3 key metrics**
3. **Challenge:** 2–3 sentences, with the baseline numbers.
4. **Strategy:** what was decided and why (3 bullets).
5. **Execution:** channels, creative samples and tools (images, carousel).
6. **Results:** a before/after chart and metrics with the measurement method and period stated.
7. **Client quote:** with name and role, and only with permission.
8. **What we'd do next / lessons learned:** builds credibility.
9. **CTA:** "Want results like these? Book a free growth audit."

**Launch set:** 6 case studies, one per major discipline (paid media, SEO, social, brand, PR, analytics), covering at least 3 industries.

**Credibility rules:**
- State the time frame and data source for every metric.
- Never round in your own favour.
- Anonymise rather than invent.

## 12. Performance, SEO, accessibility and responsive standards

**Performance budgets** (75th percentile, mobile)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| LCP | ≤ 2.0 s |
| INP | ≤ 150 ms |
| CLS | ≤ 0.05 |
| Total JS on first load | ≤ 150 KB gzip |
| Homepage weight | ≤ 1 MB |
| Lighthouse (all four categories) | ≥ 95 |

**How to meet them**
- **Build:** a static or SSG framework (Astro or Next.js static export) on a CDN with edge caching. HTML is pre-rendered, and interactive islands hydrate only when visible.
- **Images:** AVIF/WebP via `<picture>` with responsive `srcset`, explicit width and height, and lazy loading below the fold. The LCP image is preloaded with `fetchpriority="high"`.
- **Fonts:** at most 2 families, self-hosted, subset WOFF2, `font-display: swap` and the main face preloaded.
- **CSS and scripts:** critical CSS is inlined and the rest deferred. No heavy sliders.
- **Third-party scripts:** analytics, chat and pixels go through a tag manager loaded after interaction or idle time, or via server-side tracking.
- **Monitoring:** real-user monitoring (CrUX or a RUM tool) plus a Lighthouse CI budget check on every deploy.

**SEO**
- One H1 per page and a logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Descriptive, keyword-led titles (≤ 60 characters) and unique meta descriptions (≤ 155 characters).
- Clean URLs (as in the sitemap), canonical tags, XML sitemap, robots.txt and hreflang if bilingual (EN/AR).
- **Schema:** `Organization`, `LocalBusiness` (if there is a physical office), `Service`, `FAQPage`, `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Review` (genuine only).
- **Internal linking:** service ↔ case study ↔ blog cluster. Each service page is the pillar for its blog cluster.
- **E-E-A-T:** author bios with credentials, dated updates, sources cited and real team photos.
- Open Graph and Twitter cards on every page, with branded OG images.

**Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)**
- Colour contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for UI elements. Don't rely on colour alone in charts.
- Fully keyboard navigable with a visible focus state, a skip link, and a mega-menu that follows the ARIA disclosure pattern.
- Form fields have labels, errors are announced with `aria-live`, and fields use autocomplete attributes.
- Images have alt text, videos have captions and transcripts, and nothing autoplays with sound.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Tap targets ≥ 44 px, and text can be resized to 200% without breaking the layout.
- Publish an accessibility statement page.

**Responsive design**
- Mobile-first breakpoints: 360 / 768 / 1024 / 1440.
- Fluid type with `clamp()` and a 12-column grid that collapses to 4 columns on mobile.
- Tables become cards on mobile, the sticky CTA bar appears on mobile only, and there is no horizontal scrolling at 320 px.

**Analytics and conversion tracking**
- GA4 with events for `cta_click`, `form_start`, `form_submit`, `audit_booked`, `asset_download`, `tool_used`, `scroll_75` and `outbound_click`.
- UTM conventions documented, consent mode v2 with a cookie banner, and a CRM integration for leads.
- Monthly CRO tests on the hero, CTA wording and form length.

## 13. Visual direction (neutral, professional)

- **Palette:**
  - Ink `#0F172A` (text/headers)
  - Slate `#475569` (secondary text)
  - Paper `#FFFFFF` / Mist `#F5F7FA` (surfaces)
  - **One accent**, e.g. Deep Teal `#0F766E` or Signal Blue `#2563EB`, for CTAs and charts
  - Success green `#16A34A` for positive metrics only
- **Typography:** *Inter* (UI and body) + *Fraunces* or *Source Serif 4* for editorial headlines, or *Inter* alone for a cleaner look. Base size 16–18 px, line height 1.6.
- **Imagery:** real team and client work, clean data visualisations and minimal stock photography. Use subtle motion only, such as metric count-ups, chart reveals and card hover lift.
- **Components:** stat cards, case-study cards, testimonial cards, service cards, a comparison table, FAQ accordion, CTA band and a 6-field form.

- **Quarterly review:** prune or merge underperforming posts, refresh the top 20% of articles and double down on the themes that convert.
