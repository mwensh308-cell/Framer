Glyph Animals

Creative coding · HTML Canvas · JavaScript Moth species live

A full-screen browser new-tab page. Flocks of animals made only of keyboard characters fly across a dark grid. Every animal is drawn from a shape function, one frame at a time: no images, sprites or libraries, just one HTML file. The moths behind this portfolio are the first species: they flutter, glide, leave fading trails and scatter when you click.
